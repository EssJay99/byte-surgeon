Summary
==============================================================================
- MmapScanner outperforms DiskScanner by ~2x for files > 128KB
- Parallel carving achieves 2.5-3x speedup over serial for large workloads
- Signature identification is O(n) over ~200 entries; single-identify < 60ns
- NTFS parsing is slower than FAT32/ext4 due to MFT record overhead
- SectorCache hit latency ~130ns, miss ~95ns, stats ~50ns
- Cache eviction is O(n) over entry count; consider LRU optimization for large caches
